Privacy Policy — Sai Sandesh

Last updated: October 2026

Sai Sandesh is a free devotional app. Your privacy matters, so here is the whole policy in plain language:

We collect nothing

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you or your device.

Network use

The app fetches devotional content (daily messages, topics, search results, share-card images) from our own server. These requests contain no personal identifiers — only the content you asked for, such as a date or a search query.

On-device data

When you share a share-card image, it is downloaded to the app's temporary cache on your device so the system share sheet can attach it. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Children

The app contains only devotional content suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please reach out through the Sai Sandesh project contact you received the app from.
